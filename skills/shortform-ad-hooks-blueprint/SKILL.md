# Shortform Ad Hooks & Narrative Remix Blueprint

Craft and systematize high-retention shortform product and app ads (`6–35s`, `9:16` vertical) without wasting production budget or hallucinating product behavior. Whenever real brand, detail-page, or screen-capture footage exists, preserve real physical truth in the product proof shots while crafting scroll-stopping narrative openers (`0–5s` A/B/C hooks OR `12–15s` 3-stage problem escalations) via **Google Flow**.

> **Clean Room Rule**: Production writers and prompt engineers use THIS file (`SKILL.md`). NEVER open `reference-breakdown.md` during active ad production, and never copy brand names, punchlines, or scene sequences from past campaigns.

## 1. Choosing Between Two Proven Architectures

### Mode 1A — The 5-Second A/B/C Hook-Only Variable Remix (`abc_remix`, ~25s)

Isolate **one fair scientific variable** when testing multiple cold openings atop a `20s` identical product proof body (`shared_body_cuts`):

- **Independent Variable (`0.0–5.0s` / `8.0–12.0s` Orthogonal A/B/C Hook Differentiation Matrix)**:
  Never let Track A, B, and C slide into paraphrases of the same PAS/spec structure (**anti-pattern: A becoming a 1:1 metaphor + 15s spec list, or B collapsing into a dim-light version of C's pain point**), and NEVER reskin one Track A template across products. Enforce 3 strictly orthogonal genres verified by `ad_gate_a_lint.py --compare-abc` (`track_archetype`) + `--compare-campaigns`:
  1. **Track A — Non-Sequitur Genre Hijacking & Absurd Reversal (`A_bgrade_reversal`, 무맥락 반전 원리 — `bgrade-reversal-ad` & 고집돌우럭 원칙 결합)**:
     - **BAN infomercial spec-list payoffs** ("선반에 두기 딱 좋아요", "가성비 끝장" 나열 금지), BAN early product clues (`product_taboo_keywords` 0% before twist), and **BAN Cross-Campaign Reskinning** (NEVER clone `"속보입니다"` / `"단 한 집만 평온"` / `"온 동네가"` disaster-news framing + `Turntable Scratch` from prior campaigns!).
     - Execute the **3-Part Non-Sequitur Reversal Principle**:
       1. **Alien Genre Hijacking (`a_genre_archetype`)**: Rotate to a genuinely fresh genre (`noir_interrogation`, `romance_breakup`, `historical_drama`, `sports_var`, `courtroom_trial`, `sci_fi_contact`, `audition_judge`, `horror_mystery`, `mock_documentary`, `absurd_skit`) completely alien to household/cleaning/scent categories; never repeat an `a_genre_archetype` across consecutive campaigns.
       2. **Physical/Logic Inversion**: Conceal the product category and transpose **one unique physical truth** of the product (e.g., rapid capillary trace removal, 90° unspillable solid stability) as the absurd dramatic stakes of that hijacked genre (`45–60%` of total runtime).
       3. **Genre-Matched Pattern-Interrupt -> Deadpan Real-Product Landing (`<= 55%`)**: Snap with a genre-matched break (`0.18–0.32s` `silence_gap` + genre-appropriate SFX/BGM drop — e.g. interrogation gavel, heartbreak violin snap, referee whistle, not locked to `Turntable Scratch`) into serene, concise real-product payoff + cheeky callback (`comment_engagement`).
  2. **Track B — 50% Offset Informational Hook (`B_info_hook_offset`, 맥락 오차율 50% 정보성 훅 — `b-info-hook` 원칙 결합)**:
     - Target semantic similarity **`hook_similarity_score` = `0.40–0.60`** between opening subject and product USP (**BAN `>= 0.70` too-close setups** like opening directly on water droplets/sprays, and **BAN PAS problem framing** like `"번지고"`, `"쏟아본 적"`, `"고민이시죠"`, `"바꿨어요"` in `0–6s`).
     - Open on a fascinating unrelated biology/physics/history fact + concrete number thread (`"6억 년 살아남은 해면동물"`, `"다리 없는 나무의 피톤치드 방어"`), step across `1–2` smooth bridges (`adjacent sim >= 0.70`) approaching the anchor -> reveal the product name for the FIRST time right on the payoff proof (`"~습니다"` calm documentary VO, Arabic numerals in concise captions, max `<= 2` spec `header_tag` banners).
  3. **Track C — Direct Routine & Kinetic Proof (`C_direct_proof`, 실용/루틴 직격 PAS형)**:
     - Classic bright unpolished POV pain opener -> immediate physical reset -> jump straight into the real product's **strongest kinetic proof cut FIRST** + 2–3 verified `[F##]` benefits + CTA. Keep PAS and spec persuasion strictly inside Track C.
- **Controlled Body (`5.0–25.0s` in `abc_remix`, or shared payoff cuts in `narrative_master`)**: Keep product proof honest & factual across `A`, `B`, `C`. Build/QC in order **`C -> B -> A`**.

### Mode 1B — 30-Second 3-Stage Narrative Escalation Master (`v2_narrative_master`, ~30s)

Use whenever a `2–3s` hook feels abrupt, gimmicky, or context-starved — when viewers need **earned empathy and escalation** before seeing the product solution:

1. **Act 1 — 3-Stage Problem Escalation (`0.0–13.5s` / `12–15s`, Google Flow + Studio Korean VO)**:
   - **Stage 1 (`0–5s`) — Night/Moment Trigger + Opportunity Cost**: Relatable setting (`11 PM craving`), concrete numbers (`최소 주문 + 52분 대기 + 배달비 4,000원`). Visuals maintain authentic handheld POV without UI clutter.
   - **Stage 2 (`5–9s`) — Physical Spatial Friction**: Stepping toward the obstacle (`원룸 1구 인덕션 + 싱크대 설거지 산더미`), visually proving why "just cook it at home" also feels heavy.
   - **Stage 3 (`9–13.5s`) — Quantified Labor Punchline**: Tactile frustration prop (`고무장갑`) + sharp contrast ratio (`"3분 라면 하나 먹자고 설거지 네 개에 47분 중노동입니다"`).
2. **Pre-Twist Dead-Stop (`0.20–0.30s` `silence_gap`)**:
   - Hard stop BGM via scratch/stop SFX + dimmed still frame right after the punchline so the ear resets completely.
3. **Act 2 — Cause-and-Effect Product Payoff (`13.5–31s`, 100% Real Client Footage + 3-Bus Sound Drop)**:
   - **Cognitive Flip (`13.5–15.6s`)**: Visual match back to single-burner obstacle (`"그래서 저는 그냥 주방을 포기했습니다"`).
   - **Activation Beat Drop (`15.6s+`)**: Full drum/bass/keys BGM drops cleanly on Beat 1 + tactile LFE thump right as user plugs in cord & turns dial (`"코드만 꽂고 다이얼 돌리면 스텐 내솥이 바로 팔팔 끓거든요"`).
   - **Friction Reversal (`21–28s`)**: Unplug-and-carry -> silicone cover rinse -> multi-use 4-in-1 montage directly answering Stage 2/3 dishwashing pain.
   - **Time-Saved Payoff + Comment CTA (`28–31s`)**: Call back to Stage 1's opening number (`"배달 52분 기다릴 시간에 이미 다 먹고 누웠습니다"`) -> single-keyword Comment-DM trigger.

### Alternative Campaign Modes

- **Mode 2 — Real-UI / App Decision Ad (`6s` Bumper or `15s` Decision Spine)**: Real screen captures only (`ADB` / native capture); never ask AI video tools to hallucinate readable Korean UI text or prices.
- **Mode 3 — Full-AI Concept Ad (`15–25s`)**: When client footage does not exist yet; keep disclosure transparent (`ai_assisted`).

## 2. Product Truth Ledger & Advertising Law Guardrails (`Gate F`)

Every claim in hooks, narration, captions, and CTAs MUST trace to `[F01..]` verified entries in `00_product_facts.md`:

1. **Zero Fabricated Claims**: Never invent specs, wattage, materials, certifications, or percentages not visibly proven in source footage or client docs (`"실리콘 커버로 물 세척 부담 완화"`, never unverified `"IPX8 완전 방수"`).
2. **Forbidden Unverified Superlatives (`표시광고법`)**: Block `완벽 방수`, `100% 효과`, `기적의`, `국내 최초`, `세계 유일`, `부작용 제로`, `무조건` unless verified under `## Whitelisted Claims`.
3. **Unconfirmed Promo Terms**: Never guess discount % or dates; keep placeholders (`단 O일 한정 공구가`) until confirmed.
4. **Disclosure Mandatory Check & Pure Organic Review Mode**: Declare `disclosure` (`paid_ad`, `sponsored`, `ai_assisted`, `own_brand`, `none`) so top-left badges render unobstructed. When the creator requests an unpaid/pure organic review (`ai_assisted` for AI buildup scenes), strictly **prohibit** `자사제품`, `공구 진행중`, fake discount percentages, and synthetic urgency; render `AI 연출 포함` top-left only and end with a conversational question CTA (`cta.type: "comment_engagement"`, `show_from_sec: total_dur - 3.8`).
5. **Direct-Frame Product Printing & Zero-Digit AI Prop Verification**: Always inspect extracted real frames (`extract-frame` / `extract-strip`) via `read_file` BEFORE naming printed product brands, dial scales, or physical specs — never assume verbal nicknames match printed housing labels. When generating AI lifestyle shots featuring dials/clocks/displays without image injection, explicitly prompt `"completely blank smooth dial/display surface with strictly ZERO numbers, ZERO digits, ZERO text marks"` so AI never invents garbled digits beside real close-up footage.

## 3. Source Compression, Full-Bleed `9:16` Framing & Kinetic Captions

Raw phone or detail-page clips vary widely (`1:1`, `3:4`, `16:9`, VFR `.mov`, extensionless mobile files, baked lower-third captions). Normalize all raw client clips to CFR `30fps` H.264 first (`ad_source_analyzer.py normalize-cfr`), and blend multi-source cuts (`type: "source"` + secondary `"type": "video"`) with per-aspect framing:

### Framing Selection Rules

1. **`smart-crop` (`9:16` Edge-to-Edge Vertical — Default for `3:4` Portrait & Narrow Single-Subject Hero Cuts)**:
   - Strip client bottom subtitles with `bottom_crop_px` (e.g., `152–162px` on `1080x1080`), then crop cleanly to `1080x1920` around tuned `crop_x_ratio` (`0.42–0.58`) with subtle `punch_zoom: 1.04` on hero close-ups. Avoid cutting awkwardly mid-cut: inspect cut boundaries (`p01_check` contact frames) so segments never leak 0.1s into adjacent source scenes.
2. **`blur-bleed` (Mandatory for `1:1` Gauges/Dials & Wide Multi-Object Montages)**:
   - **Never apply `smart-crop` to `1:1` dials, gauges, or wide props** where horizontal zoom clips bezel numbers or hands (`40..55` / multi-cut collage). Place the sharp trimmed frame centered (e.g., `card_y: 330`) over a `gblur=sigma=32` background. Keep that cue's caption `y_pos` cleanly outside the card bottom border (`y_pos: 1385`) so text never crosses the sharp frame edge.
3. **Native `9:16` Flow Guard & Pre-Assembly Strip Inspection**: On every AI Flow cut (`V1..V3`), explicitly set `"framing": "full-bleed", "bottom_crop_px": 0` so root `bottom_crop_px` never crops native `9:16` footage, keep `duration * speed <= actual_clip_duration`, and inspect `_qc/*_strip.png` via `read_file` BEFORE assembly to trim late-frame AI morph glitches (`src_start`/`duration`).
4. **`editorial-card`**: Dark canvas `#12141A` top-header/center-card layout reserved for dense comparative info cards or explicit side-by-side comparison creatives.

### Reels Kinetic Typography & Verbatim Sync (`1080x1920`)

- **Kinetic Style (`reels_kinetic`)**: Crisp white bold type (`stroke_width=8`, no heavy dark box) + `**keyword**` yellow (`#FFE047`) punch words + compact red-dot `pop_badge` pill above captions.
- **Constraints**: Max `2` lines, `<= 22` plain chars/line, `>= 1.2s` dwell time per cue, zero emojis, keep critical UI inside `x: 64..880, y: 160..1580` (clear of right Like/Share rail & bottom drawer).
- **Audio-First Verbatim Rule**: Always run `ad_audio_prep.py` + listen to the actual generated Korean VO before writing/locking captions and source cut durations — NEVER ship stale script copy that differs from what the voice actor actually spoke.

## 4. 3-Bus Studio Sound Architecture & Safe `-14.0 LUFS` Mastering

- **Mute Original Client BGM (`audio_owner: "mute"`)**: Do NOT slice choppy source music across rapid cuts. Mute all cut segments and rebuild sound cleanly on 3 discrete buses:
  1. **Bus 1 (`vo_audio`)**: Studio Korean VO (`Omni 1.2 Flash` dry vocal booth, dead-air trimmed & RMS-balanced via `ad_audio_prep.py --manifest`, filtered with `highpass=85` + broadcast compression).
  2. **Bus 2 (`bgm_timeline`)**: 2-stage continuous loop stems keyed by VO `sidechaincompress`: sparse keys during Act 1 (`-15 dB`) -> full drop to silence during `0.26s` twist freeze -> sparse bass/keys recovery -> full 3-stem groove drop (`-10.8 dB`, `stem_offset_sec: 0.0`) right on Act 2 activation.
  3. **Bus 3 (`sfx_events`)**: Sample-accurate stereo Foley & transition hits (`Bell Small`, `Dishes`, `Turntable Scratch` pre-twist, `Air Reverse` + `LFE Impact` on drop, soft `Boiling Water` under boiling pot, `Bell Transition` on CTA).
- **Mandatory 2-Pass Limiter**: Never run raw `loudnorm` over hard silence gaps (`NaN` failure); run 2-pass `ebur128` -> `volume + alimiter` -> `ebur128` (`-14.0 LUFS`).
- **Pre-Ship Real Playback Audit**: Always watch & listen to the final rendered `.mp4` end-to-end via `read_file` before declaring done.

## 5. 2-Axis KPI Experimentation Matrix & Comment-DM Scale-Up

- **Axis 1 — Attention & Hold (`A` / Narrative Hook strength)**: 3-second hold rate (`>= 35%`), completion through Act 1 twist (`>= 13-15s`), shares/sends.
- **Axis 2 — Purchase Intent (`C` / Action Proof strength)**: Comment-keyword DM conversions per 1K views, saves, ROAS.
- **High-Conversion Comment-DM or Organic Comment CTA**: Single short `2–4` syllable keyword (`댓글에 '키워드' 남기면 링크 DM`) for conversion ads, OR an authentic open question (`cta.type: "comment_engagement"`) for pure reviews, mirrored verbatim in rendered badge, caption line 1, and pinned comment.

## 6. 9:16 + 1:1 Profile-Grid Cover Thumbnail Design (`ad_thumbnail_builder.py`)

Design dedicated `1080x1920` cover frames tailored to each hook archetype rather than grabbing random dark frames:

- **Strict Cross-Grid Safe Zone (`1:1` `y=420..1500`, `3:4` `y=240..1680`, Zero-Clip Strict Box `x=56..1024, y=460..1460`)**: Instagram Reels crop profile grids to both `1:1` (`y=420..1500`) and `3:4` (`y=240..1680`). Enable `"strict_margin": true` so every pill badge, oversized headline box, and rounded product inset card stays strictly inside `x in [56, 1024], y in [460, 1460]` — **ZERO top-line half-slicing or left/right clipping** on profile grid tiles (`40px` buffer inside `1:1`).
- **Mobile-Grid Oversized Typography (`picturebooksub` Standard, `114–134px`)**: Headlines must read effortlessly at `160px` thumbnail grid width (`font_size: 118–132`, automatic horizontal width fit inside `968px`, max `2–3` crisp short lines filling `~78–88%` width). Support 3 high-contrast styles in `ad_thumbnail_builder.py`:
  - **`stroke_bold`**: Oversized white/yellow heavy-stroke text (`stroke_w: 11`) + scrim/shadow — ideal for Track A split/drama covers.
  - **`panel_card`**: Rounded semi-opaque dark backdrop card wrapping bright headlines — ideal for Track B info-hook/science covers.
  - **`line_blocks`**: Tight solid white/accent rounded blocks per headline line with bold ink text — ideal for Track C practical punch headlines & inset proof covers.
- **Clean Typography & Pre-Ship Visual QC**: Zero meta-labels (`A안`/`B안`), zero emojis, subtle `1:1` cyan + `3:4` amber + vertical safe-margin guide lines rendered **ONLY on the QA contact sheet** (`thumbnails_sheet.png`) — inspect both sheet and covers via `read_file`.
